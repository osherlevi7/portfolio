Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

1. Introduction

LionFish Consultant ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our IT, DevOps, and Cybersecurity services.

As a provider of healthcare IT and cybersecurity services, we understand the critical importance of data protection and comply with applicable privacy laws including HIPAA, GDPR, and other relevant regulations.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

  • Name, email address, phone number, and company information
  • Business contact details and professional credentials
  • Service preferences and project requirements
  • Communication history and support requests

2.2 Technical Information

  • IP addresses and device information
  • Website usage data and analytics
  • System logs and performance metrics
  • Security event data and audit trails

2.3 Healthcare Data (HIPAA Protected)

  • Patient data when providing healthcare IT services
  • Medical device information and configurations
  • PACS/RIS/EMR system data
  • Compliance documentation and audit records

3. How We Use Your Information

3.1 Service Provision

  • Deliver IT, DevOps, and Cybersecurity services
  • Provide technical support and troubleshooting
  • Manage infrastructure and system monitoring
  • Ensure compliance with security standards

3.2 Communication

  • Respond to inquiries and support requests
  • Send service updates and security notifications
  • Provide training and documentation
  • Share relevant industry insights and best practices

3.3 Security and Compliance

  • Implement security controls and monitoring
  • Conduct risk assessments and audits
  • Ensure HIPAA and GDPR compliance
  • Maintain incident response capabilities

4. Data Protection and Security

4.1 Security Measures

  • Encryption of data in transit and at rest
  • Multi-factor authentication and access controls
  • Regular security assessments and penetration testing
  • 24/7 monitoring and incident response

4.2 HIPAA Compliance

  • Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) with healthcare clients
  • Administrative, physical, and technical safeguards
  • Regular HIPAA training for our team
  • Audit trails and access logging

4.3 GDPR Compliance

  • Data minimization and purpose limitation
  • Right to access, rectification, and erasure
  • Data portability and processing restrictions
  • Cross-border data transfer safeguards

5. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: Trusted partners who assist in service delivery
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or legal process
  • Security Incidents: To investigate and respond to security threats
  • Business Transfers: In connection with business mergers or acquisitions

6. Data Retention

  • Active Services: Retained for the duration of service provision
  • Healthcare Data: Retained as required by HIPAA regulations
  • Security Logs: Retained for incident investigation and compliance
  • Marketing Data: Retained until consent is withdrawn

7. Your Rights and Choices

7.1 Access and Control

  • Access your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Object to processing of your data

7.2 Communication Preferences

  • Opt-out of marketing communications
  • Choose communication channels
  • Set frequency preferences
  • Update contact information

8. International Data Transfers

As a globally distributed team operating from the U.S. and Israel, we may transfer your information internationally. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, including:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs)
  • Adequacy decisions and certifications
  • Encryption and security measures
  • Regular compliance assessments

9. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Improve website functionality and user experience
  • Analyze website traffic and performance
  • Provide personalized content and services
  • Ensure security and prevent fraud

10. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected information from a child, please contact us immediately.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by:

  • Posting the updated policy on our website
  • Sending email notifications to registered users
  • Updating the "Last updated" date
  • Providing advance notice for significant changes
